All Six Trig Functions

Beyond sine and cosine, four more functions complete the picture. Each is the reciprocal of one of the primary three, with its own domain restrictions and asymptotes.

The Six Functions

Starting from a right triangle with opposite \(o\), adjacent \(a\), hypotenuse \(h\): \[\sin\theta = \frac{o}{h},\; \cos\theta = \frac{a}{h},\; \tan\theta = \frac{o}{a}\] \[\csc\theta = \frac{h}{o},\; \sec\theta = \frac{h}{a},\; \cot\theta = \frac{a}{o}\]

Tangent has vertical asymptotes wherever \(\cos\theta = 0\) (i.e. \(\theta = \pi/2 + k\pi\)). Secant is the reciprocal of cosine and shares those asymptotes. Cosecant has asymptotes where \(\sin\theta = 0\). Cotangent has asymptotes where \(\sin\theta = 0\).

Key Formulas

Reciprocal Identities
\[ \csc\theta = \frac{1}{\sin\theta}, \quad \sec\theta = \frac{1}{\cos\theta}, \quad \cot\theta = \frac{1}{\tan\theta} \]
Quotient Identities
\[ \tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}, \qquad \cot\theta = \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta} \]
Pythagorean Forms
\[ 1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta, \quad 1 + \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta \]
Periods
\[ \sin, \cos, \csc, \sec \text{ have period } 2\pi; \quad \tan, \cot \text{ have period } \pi \]

Worked Examples

Example 1 — Finding all six from one value
Given \(\sin\theta = 3/5\) and \(\cos\theta = 4/5\), find all six trig values
1
\(\tan\theta = (3/5)/(4/5) = 3/4\)
2
\(\csc\theta = 5/3,\; \sec\theta = 5/4,\; \cot\theta = 4/3\)
✓
All six: \(\sin=3/5,\,\cos=4/5,\,\tan=3/4,\,\csc=5/3,\,\sec=5/4,\,\cot=4/3\)

Key Takeaways

◈

Cosecant, secant, and cotangent are simply reciprocals — no new information, just new notation.

◈

Tangent and cotangent have period \(\pi\); the other four have period \(2\pi\).

◈

Vertical asymptotes occur where the denominator (sin or cos) equals zero.

Laws of Sines & Cosines

The Law of Sines and Law of Cosines extend trigonometry beyond right triangles, letting you solve any triangle given enough information. Drag the triangle vertices below to see both laws update live.

When to Use Each Law

Law of Sines — use when you know two angles and a side (AAS / ASA), or two sides and a non-included angle (SSA — the "ambiguous case").

Law of Cosines — use when you know two sides and the included angle (SAS), or all three sides (SSS).

Key Formulas

Law of Sines
\[ \frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} = 2R \]
Law of Cosines
\[ c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos C \]
Area
\[ \text{Area} = \tfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C = \tfrac{1}{2}bc\sin A = \tfrac{1}{2}ac\sin B \]
Heron's Formula (SSS area)
\[ s = \tfrac{a+b+c}{2}, \quad \text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)} \]

Worked Examples

Example 1 — Law of Sines (AAS)
Triangle with \(A=50°,\,B=70°,\,a=8\). Find side \(b\).
1
\(C = 180°-50°-70° = 60°\)
2
\(\dfrac{b}{\sin 70°} = \dfrac{8}{\sin 50°}\)
✓
\(b = \dfrac{8\sin 70°}{\sin 50°} \approx 9.80\)
Example 2 — Law of Cosines (SAS)
Triangle with \(a=5,\,b=7,\,C=60°\). Find side \(c\).
1
\(c^2 = 25+49-2(5)(7)\cos60° = 74-35 = 39\)
✓
\(c = \sqrt{39} \approx 6.24\)

Key Takeaways

◈

The Law of Sines applies to all triangles, not just right triangles — but beware the ambiguous (SSA) case.

◈

The Law of Cosines reduces to the Pythagorean theorem when \(C=90°\): \(c^2=a^2+b^2\).

◈

Always verify that three angles sum to 180° as a sanity check.
